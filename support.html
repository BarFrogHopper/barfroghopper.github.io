Getting started

Who can use Bar Frog?

Adults 21 and older. You'll be asked for your birthdate when you sign up.

How do I sign up?

Create an account with your email, or use Sign in with Apple. If we email you a confirmation link, tap it and then log in.

Checking in

How do I check in?

Open the Check In tab. If you allow location, nearby bars, restaurants and stadiums appear on the map and in the list. You can also type a venue name and tap Search. Pick a place, then add drinks (with a style, e.g. "Yuengling" + "Lager"), a star rating, a review, photos and friends you're with, and tap Check In.

Do I have to be at the bar?

Location helps you find places near you, but you can also search by name. Please only check in where you actually are. Fake check-ins to game the leaderboard break our Terms.

Location isn't working

Go to iPhone Settings › Bar Frog › Location and choose While Using the App. Make sure Location Services is on (Settings › Privacy & Security › Location Services). You can always search by name instead.

How are points earned?

Every check-in earns points depending on the type of place (breweries and stadiums are worth more). You get +1 for writing a review and +1 for adding a photo.

Badges & leaderboard

How do badges work?

There are 1,087 badges, from states and cities to college bars, stadiums, airports, national parks, beaches, ski towns, wine regions, nightlife streets, festivals, holidays, bar themes and cuisines. Many have bronze, silver, gold and platinum tiers. See them under Profile › Badges.

How does the leaderboard work?

Choose Friends, Local (people near your home ZIP code) or Global, and Week, Month or All time. Your own rank stays pinned at the bottom. Business accounts aren't ranked.

Friends & sharing

How do I find friends?

Tap the person icon in the feed. Search by name or username, or tap Find friends from contacts. Phone numbers and emails are turned into one-way hashes on your phone, and your contact list is never uploaded. You can also invite friends by text.

Who can see my check-ins?

Your accepted friends, friends you tag, and the verified owner of a venue you checked in at. Your name, username, profile picture, points and badges are visible to other Bar Frog users. Details are in the Privacy Policy.

How do I share to Facebook or X?

After a check-in, tap Share (or the Facebook/X buttons) to open the iPhone share sheet and pick an app.

Safety

How do I report or block someone?

Tap the … menu on a post or next to a friend, then choose Report or Block. Blocking hides both of your content from each other and removes the friendship. You can also email us.

Business owners

How do I claim my bar or restaurant?

Sign up with a Business account, search for your venue and submit a claim with your business phone. We verify ownership (usually by calling the venue's listed phone number) before the dashboard unlocks. Once approved, you can see check-ins and reviews at your venue and reply to reviews.

Your account

How do I delete my account?

In the app: Profile › Delete Account. See full instructions, including how to request deletion by email.
