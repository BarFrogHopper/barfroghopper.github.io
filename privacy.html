Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 23, 2026

This policy explains what information the Bar Frog iPhone app ("Bar Frog," "we," "us") collects, how we use and share it, and the choices you have. Bar Frog is operated by Alfred Maurer, an independent developer based in New Jersey, USA. Questions: barfroghopper@gmail.com.

The short version: We collect what's needed to run a social check-in app: your account details, the places you check in, the reviews and photos you post, and who your friends are. We use your location to find places near you. Your contact list is hashed on your phone and never uploaded. We don't sell your data, show ads, or track you across other apps.

1. Information we collect

Information you give us

  • Account details: first and last name, email address, username, password (stored only as a secure hash by our authentication provider), home ZIP code, date of birth (to confirm you are 21+), and whether the account is personal or business. If you use Sign in with Apple, Apple shares your name and either your email or an Apple private-relay email.
  • Profile picture: a photo you upload, or an emoji or frog mascot you choose.
  • Content you post: check-ins, drinks and food you log (with styles), star ratings, reviews, review photos, friends you tag, and, for business owners, replies to reviews.
  • Social connections: friend requests, friends, and users you block.
  • Wishlist of places you want to visit.
  • Phone number (optional): if you turn on "Let friends find you by phone," we store only a one-way SHA-256 hash of your number, never the number itself.
  • Business claims: the venue, your business phone number, your account email and any notes, used to verify ownership.
  • Reports and support: reports you file about content or users, and emails you send us.

Information collected when you use the app

  • Precise location (only with your permission, only while the app is in use): when you open the Check In screen, your device's coordinates are sent to our server to look up nearby venues. We don't store your raw coordinates in your account. We do store which venue you checked in at and when, and our server logs may briefly keep an approximate search location (rounded to about 100 meters). Bar Frog does not track your location in the background and does not share your live location. Friends see the venues you check in at, not your GPS position.
  • Venue searches: search text is sent to our server and cached (without your user ID) to make searches faster. We keep a timestamp of each search tied to your account to prevent abuse (rate limiting).
  • Points, badges and leaderboard rank, calculated from your check-ins.
  • Basic technical data: like any online service, our hosting provider records technical logs (such as IP address, timestamps and request details) for security and troubleshooting.

Contacts (optional)

If you tap Find friends from contacts and allow access, Bar Frog reads the phone numbers and emails in your address book on your phone and turns each one into a one-way SHA-256 hash. Only these hashes are sent to our server, where they are compared against the hashes of Bar Frog users' emails and opted-in phone numbers. The hashes from your contacts are not stored, and your contacts' names, numbers and emails are never uploaded. Invitations you send go out through your phone's Messages app or share sheet.

Because matching uses email hashes, someone who has your email address in their contacts can find your Bar Frog profile. Phone matching only happens if you turn it on.

Camera and photo library

We access the camera or photos only when you choose to add a review photo or profile picture. Only the photos you pick are uploaded.

What we don't collect

We don't use advertising identifiers, analytics or ad SDKs, or cross-app tracking, and we don't collect health, financial or browsing data.

2. How we use information

  • To create and secure your account, sign you in, and confirm you are 21 or older.
  • To show nearby venues, record check-ins, and build your map, wishlist, points, badges and leaderboard rank (your home ZIP code powers the "Local" leaderboard).
  • To power social features: your feed, friend suggestions, tagging, profiles and sharing.
  • To let verified venue owners see activity at their venue and reply to reviews.
  • To moderate content: reviewing reports, removing content that breaks our Terms, and suspending abusive accounts.
  • To prevent fraud, spam and abuse, and to fix bugs.
  • To respond to you and send essential service messages (for example, email confirmation or password reset). We don't send marketing email.

4. How we share information

We do not sell or rent your personal information, and we don't share it for advertising. We share information only:

  • With service providers that run Bar Frog for us: Supabase (database, authentication, file storage and server functions, hosted in the United States); Google (Google Maps Platform Places API), which receives the location or search text of venue lookups from our server, not your name or account; and Apple (Sign in with Apple, and Apple Maps, which displays the maps in the app under Apple's privacy policy). Venue names and addresses come from Google and are shown with Google attribution.
  • With other users and venue owners, as described in section 3.
  • When you choose to share: sharing a check-in to Facebook, X, Messages or another app happens through the iOS share sheet. What you share is then governed by that service's policies.
  • For legal and safety reasons: if required by law, or to protect the rights, property or safety of our users, the public or Bar Frog.
  • Business transfer: if Bar Frog is sold or merged, information may transfer to the new owner under this policy.

5. Data retention

We keep your information while your account is active. To remove a single check-in, review or photo without deleting your account, email us and we'll delete it. When you delete your account, your profile and content are deleted right away (see what is deleted). Server logs and backups expire on their own, normally within 30 days. Moderation records (reports and blocks) may be kept in de-identified form for up to 12 months to prevent abuse. Pending business claims are deleted with the account.

6. Your choices and rights

  • Location, Camera, Photos, Contacts: change these any time in iPhone Settings › Bar Frog. The app still works without them (search venues by name instead of using location).
  • Phone matching: turn it on or off in the Friends screen.
  • Change your profile picture, remove friends and block users in the app. To remove a tag of yourself or a single post, email us.
  • Delete your account: Profile › Delete Account, or see deletion instructions.
  • Access, correction, deletion, portability: email us and we'll respond within 30 days (45 days where state law allows). Depending on where you live (for example California, Colorado, Virginia, Connecticut, other US states, the EU/UK), you may have additional rights, including the right to appeal a decision. We don't sell or "share" personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising, and we don't use it to make decisions that have legal effects about you.

7. Security

Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS) and stored with Supabase, which encrypts data at rest. Row-level security rules limit who can read each record. Passwords are hashed. No system is perfectly secure, so please use a strong, unique password and tell us right away if you suspect unauthorized access.

8. Age requirement

Bar Frog is only for adults 21 and older. It is rated 18+ on the App Store, and signup requires a birthdate showing age 21 or older. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 21. If we learn that we have, we delete the account. Please contact us if you believe someone underage is using Bar Frog.

9. International users

Bar Frog is operated from the United States and data is stored in the United States. By using the app outside the US, you understand your information will be processed in the US.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. We'll change the effective date above, and for significant changes we'll notify you in the app or by email before they take effect.
