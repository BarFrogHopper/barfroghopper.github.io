Delete your Bar Frog account

You can permanently delete your Bar Frog account and the data linked to it at any time.

Option 1: In the app (fastest)

  1. Open Bar Frog and sign in.
  2. Tap the Profile tab.
  3. Scroll down and tap DELETE ACCOUNT.
  4. Confirm by tapping Delete.

Your account is deleted right away and you're signed out.

Option 2: By email

If you can't get into the app, email barfroghopper@gmail.com from the email address on your account (or include your username) with the subject "Delete my Bar Frog account." We may ask you to confirm before deleting, to protect your account. We complete email requests within 30 days, usually much sooner.

What gets deleted

  • Your profile: name, username, email, birthdate, home ZIP code, profile picture and phone/email hashes
  • All your check-ins, drinks and food logged, reviews, star ratings and review photos
  • Friend tags, friendships and friend requests, your wishlist, points, badges and leaderboard history
  • Business data: venue claims and your replies to reviews
  • If you used Sign in with Apple, we also revoke Bar Frog's access to your Apple ID

What may be kept

  • Reports you filed about other users, and reports or blocks other users made about you, may be kept in de-identified form for up to 12 months where needed to keep the community safe and prevent abuse.
  • Cached venue information (bar names and addresses) isn't personal data and isn't deleted.
  • Copies in routine server logs or backups expire on their own, normally within 30 days.
  • Information we're legally required to keep.

Deleting the app from your phone does not delete your account. Things you shared outside Bar Frog (for example, a post on Facebook or X) must be deleted on those services.
